## 📌 Rules & Principles

1. **The Walnut Tree Principle (Consistency > Intensity):**  
   Inspired by the Persian podcast: Like the walnut tree in the garden that seemed static day by day yet grew to be the tallest, taking a step "slightly more than nothing" every single day compounds into immense, quiet strength.

2. **Celebrate Micro-Wins:**  
   Acknowledge and honor small victories every step of the way—finishing a chapter of *The Midnight Library*, completing a bioinformatics module on HarvardX, debugging a script, or reading a full research paper. Every completed piece is a milestone.

3. **Learning in Public:**  
   Document the real process, authentic struggles, working snippets, and genuine breakthroughs without fear of judgment.

4. **Execution Over Perfection:**  
   A working draft or a raw script is vastly superior to an unwritten ideal. Build the prototype first; optimize and refactor later. *(I always wanted to wait until I became a "better, more polished researcher" before documenting my journey here, but I decided to start today—embracing all my imperfections and learning openly along the way.)*

5. **Mind-Body Alignment as a Metric:**  
   Sustainable cognitive output demands daily movement, mindfulness, and rest. Wellness is a vital research pillar, not an afterthought.

6. **Honor the Low-Energy Days:**  
   On exhausting days, writing a single bullet point keeps the chain unbroken. Showing up is already a win.
